British Trade Awards — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how British Trade Awards (the “Organiser”, “we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses and protects personal data, including data processed in connection with the British Trade Awards competition and guide. It is intended to meet the transparency requirements of UK data protection legislation, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who We Are and How to Contact Us

Controller

British Trade Awards acts as the data controller for personal data processed in connection with the Awards and related website activities.

Contact details

Email: info@britishtradeawards.com

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we use your personal data, please contact us using the details above.

2. What Personal Data We Collect

We collect and process different categories of personal data depending on how you interact with us.

2.1 Website visitors and general enquiries

  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device identifiers and basic analytics data.
  • Cookies and similar technologies (see our Cookies Policy).
  • Information you provide via contact forms or email, such as your name, email address, phone number and enquiry details.

2.2 Awards entrants, nominees and winners

  • Identity details: name, business name, trading style, job title or role.
  • Contact details: business telephone number, business email address, business website URL, trading address and social media handles where applicable.
  • Category and region information, and details of your trade or services.
  • Eligibility and vetting information (e.g. public liability insurance details, qualifications, accreditations, proofs of trading address).
  • Imagery, testimonials and other materials submitted with entries or captured at Awards events (photos, video, logos, descriptions).

2.3 Voters and supporters

  • Identity: name and, where provided, relationship to the Nominee (e.g. customer, colleague).
  • Contact details: email address and, where provided, phone number.
  • Voting information (categories voted in, timestamps, technical identifiers used for anti-fraud checks).

2.4 Guide users and consumers

  • Technical data such as IP address, device information and usage data relating to searches and filters used in the British Trade Awards guide.
  • Any contact or enquiry details you provide if you get in touch with us about a listed tradesperson or the guide.

2.5 Sponsors, partners and suppliers

  • Contact information for representatives of sponsors, partners, suppliers and venues (names, roles, business contact details).
  • Contract, billing and communication records relevant to our relationship.

2.6 Job applicants and staff (summary)

For job applicants and staff, we process personal data in line with separate HR/privacy notices which are provided at the point of recruitment or employment.

3. How We Obtain Personal Data

We collect personal data in the following ways:

  • Directly from you when you submit an entry, nomination, vote, enquiry or application, or when you interact with our website, email or social media channels.
  • From publicly available sources (for example, Companies House listings, professional registers, trade accreditation bodies) when verifying eligibility and vetting entrants.
  • From partners or third-party service providers (such as ticketing or event platforms) where they process data on our behalf and share relevant information with us in line with contracts and data protection law.

Where we obtain personal data from sources other than you, we will provide you with this privacy information within a reasonable period, usually within one month or at the first point of contact, in accordance with UK GDPR Articles 13 and 14.

4. Purposes of Processing and Lawful Bases

We use personal data for specific, identified purposes and rely on one or more lawful bases for each purpose.

4.1 Running and administering the Awards

Purposes: Receiving, reviewing and managing entries and nominations; vetting eligibility, including verifying insurance, qualifications and trading status; shortlisting nominees and selecting winners (including judged and publicly-voted categories).

Lawful basis: Performance of a contract (processing entries and nominations in line with our Terms & Conditions). Legitimate interests (ensuring the integrity and credibility of the Awards).

4.2 Operating and verifying the public vote

Purposes: Running the public voting process; preventing fraud and abuse of the voting system (e.g. multiple votes, bots, scripts, organised manipulation).

Lawful basis: Legitimate interests (maintaining fairness and integrity of the Awards and protecting against abuse).

4.3 Communicating with entrants, nominees, winners and voters

Purposes: Providing updates about entries, nominations, shortlisting, events and results; responding to queries and correspondence.

Lawful basis: Performance of a contract (necessary communications about your participation). Legitimate interests (general relationship management and service administration).

4.4 Publishing nominee and winner information

Purposes: Publishing nominee and winner details on our website, in the British Trade Awards guide, and across our Publication Channels (website, social media, email newsletters, print materials and press releases); promoting recognised trades businesses and informing consumers about award outcomes.

Data involved: Business name, trading style and logo; category, region and description of trade/services; business website URL, business telephone number and business email address; imagery and other materials supplied or captured in connection with the Awards.

Lawful basis: Legitimate interests (promoting the Awards, supporting the trade sector and helping consumers identify recognised tradespeople).

4.5 Operating the British Trade Awards guide and website

Purposes: Providing a searchable guide to tradespeople who have participated in the Awards; maintaining and improving the website and user experience (including security and performance monitoring).

Lawful basis: Legitimate interests (operating our website and guide, informing the public about award participants).

4.6 Marketing and sponsorship communications

Purposes: Sending updates about future Awards, events or British Trade Awards news; sending sponsor or partner communications where appropriate.

Lawful basis: Legitimate interests (for limited B2B communications to business contacts). Consent (for certain marketing, particularly to individuals in non-business contexts or where required by electronic communications law).

You can always opt out of marketing communications by following the unsubscribe link or contacting us using the details in section 1.

4.7 Legal, regulatory and risk management

Purposes: Keeping appropriate records to comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations; handling complaints, disputes or claims; cooperating with regulators and law-enforcement where necessary.

Lawful basis: Legal obligation. Legitimate interests (defending our rights and managing risk).

5. British Trade Awards Guide and Website Listings

The British Trade Awards website, including any search tools, maps, filters or “British Trade Awards Find a Tradesperson” listings, is provided for general information only. It helps members of the public identify tradespeople who have participated in the Awards, but it does not constitute professional advice or a recommendation to select any particular tradesperson.

British Trade Awards is not a party to any contract between consumers and tradespeople identified through the website or guide and has no control over, and accepts no responsibility or liability for, any quotations, advice, workmanship, services or outcomes arising from such engagements. All contracts for work are entered into directly between the consumer and the tradesperson.

Consumers remain solely responsible for carrying out their own checks (including identity, qualifications, insurance, references and suitability) before instructing any tradesperson and for complying with their own legal obligations. Use of the guide or any website listing does not replace independent enquiries or due diligence.

6. Data Sharing and Recipients

We do not sell personal data. We may share personal data with:

  • Service providers who host our website, manage email and voting platforms, provide analytics, or support events and PR activities, under appropriate contracts.
  • Sponsors and partners, where necessary to administer sponsorship arrangements or co-branded activities, and where a lawful basis for any direct communications exists.
  • Professional advisers (lawyers, accountants, insurers) where necessary for advice or risk management.
  • Regulators and authorities, such as the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) or law-enforcement bodies, where we are required or permitted by law to share information.

When naming specific service providers or categories would improve transparency (for example, a particular voting platform or hosting provider), we may list them in this Policy or in a separate services appendix.

7. International Transfers

If we transfer personal data outside the UK (for example, because a cloud or email service provider hosts data in another country), we will ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with UK data protection law. This may include adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses or international data transfer agreements.

We will provide further details on request about any relevant international transfers and safeguards.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as is reasonably necessary for the purposes set out in this Policy, and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. Indicative retention periods include:

  • Awards entries, nominations, vetting records and voting data: typically up to 6 years from the end of the relevant Awards year, to align with common limitation and accounting periods, unless a longer period is justified by ongoing disputes or legal obligations.
  • Nominee and Winner listings (including published contact details): normally for the Awards year plus an archival period (e.g. up to 6 years) to preserve an accurate record of Awards outcomes and support sector recognition. Minimal records of Award outcomes (such as business name, category and year) may be kept for historical and audit purposes beyond that time.
  • General website enquiries and communications: typically up to 3 years from last contact, unless required longer for legal reasons.
  • Sponsor, partner and supplier records: in line with contract and accounting requirements, typically up to 6 years after the relationship ends.

We may adjust specific retention periods as our operations or legal requirements change, and will reflect such changes in this Policy.

9. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction. Measures may include:

  • secure hosting and encryption for certain data in transit and at rest;
  • access controls and role-based permissions;
  • regular backups and secure disposal of data;
  • staff training on data protection and confidentiality;
  • policies and procedures for incident response and data protection by design and by default.

However, no system can be completely secure. You should take care when sending information to us over the internet and use up-to-date security software and practices.

10. Your Data Protection Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Right of access – to obtain a copy of your personal data and information about how it is used.
  • Right to rectification – to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure – in certain circumstances, to request deletion of your data.
  • Right to restrict processing – in certain circumstances, to request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability – to receive certain data in a structured, commonly used format and transmit it to another controller.
  • Right to object – to object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.

Where we rely on consent for specific processing, you also have the right to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

You can exercise your rights by contacting us using the details in section 1. We may need to verify your identity before responding, and we will reply within the timeframes required by law (normally one month).

If you are unhappy with how we handle your personal data, please contact us first so we can try to resolve your concerns. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) or another competent supervisory authority. Details are available at www.ico.org.uk.

11. Awards-Specific Privacy Section

In addition to the general information above, this Awards-specific section summarises the main ways we use personal data in connection with the British Trade Awards competition and guide.

11.1 Publication of nominee and winner details

For shortlisted Nominees and Winners, we may publish:

  • business name and trading style;
  • category and region;
  • business website URL, business telephone number and business email address;
  • logo and brief description of trade/services;
  • awards-related photos and other imagery.

This information may appear on the British Trade Awards website, in the guide, on social media, in email newsletters, printed materials and press releases.

We normally publish only business-related contact details. Where contact details relate to a sole trader or individual (for example, a personal mobile number or personal email address used for business), they may be published as part of their business contact information unless the individual requests an alternative contact detail.

11.2 Legitimate interests

We rely on legitimate interests as our lawful basis for publishing nominee and winner details and operating the Awards guide. Our legitimate interests include:

  • promoting the Awards and recognised trades businesses;
  • providing consumers with transparent information about award outcomes;
  • supporting the trades sector and public recognition of excellence.

We have considered the impact on individuals and concluded that these interests are not overridden by the rights and freedoms of data subjects, particularly given the professional context, limited nature of the data published and ability to request correction or removal of certain personal contact details.

11.3 Requests to amend or remove published details

Entrants, Nominees and Winners may request:

  • correction of published details, including contact details;
  • removal of particular personal contact details (such as a personal mobile number);
  • reduction of a listing to a minimal record (for example, business name, category and year only).

We will consider such requests in line with UK data protection law and our legitimate interests in maintaining an accurate historical record of the Awards. Minimal records of Award outcomes may be retained even where some details are removed.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our activities, legal requirements or guidance from the ICO.

We will publish the updated Policy on our website with a revised “last updated” date. If changes are material, we may also notify you directly where appropriate (for example, by email to entrants or nominees).
