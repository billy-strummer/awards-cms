Email Sequences

Create automated email sequences triggered by status changes or manual enrollment. Use sequences for multi-email workflows; use the Email Builder for one-off campaigns.

Common sequences:
  • Entry received → Confirmation email → Payment reminder (3 days) → Voting reminder (when live)
  • Winner announced → Congratulations → Media pack → Post-event survey (1 week)
  • Sponsor onboarded → Welcome & overview → Event logistics → Upgrade offer (30 days)
