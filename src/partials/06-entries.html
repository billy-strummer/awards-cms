Getting Started — Entry Submissions

How entries flow into your awards programme.

1
Create Award Categories
Go to Awards and set up your categories first — entrants select a category when submitting.
2
Share the Entry Form
Copy the public entry form URL and share it with entrants, or bulk-import nominees per county/city from the Award Areas tab. There is no manual "Add Entry" button here — entries come from the public form or an Award Areas CSV import.
3
Review & Progress Entries
Update status (Under Review → Shortlisted → Winner, or Rejected) and assign entries to judges.
