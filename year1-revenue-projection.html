01 Executive Summary

Central Year 1 estimate: approximately £1.34 million, within a genuine range of roughly £430,000 (low) to £3.27 million (high).

This is a wide range on purpose. The figure is driven almost entirely by three assumptions this report cannot calculate for you — how many of the 62 categories genuinely have credible local nominees in every location in year one, how quickly a brand-new (unproven) awards programme achieves purchase uptake on winner-stage products, and how many regional ceremonies the business can realistically run in its first year. Every number below is shown with its underlying calculation, so the model can be adjusted as real assumptions become clearer.

No revenue is included from entry fees (confirmed free-to-enter), or from directory, membership, or pay-per-lead streams — these depend on the programme already having a track record, which by definition it won't have in Year 1.

110 locations × 62 categories = 6,820 location-category slots — the absolute ceiling on how many individual award outcomes could exist in Year 1, before any realism adjustment is applied.

03 Methodology — The Funnel

Revenue is built up through a funnel, not applied as a flat rate to the ceiling above:

  • Step 1 — Active slots: 6,820 theoretical slots × a category fill rate (45–80%, see assumptions below) = the number of category-location combinations that genuinely run with real nominees in Year 1.
  • Step 2 — Winners: exactly one winner per active slot, by definition.
  • Step 3 — Total nominees: active slots × an average nominee count per slot (4–8, not always the full 10 in a new programme) = total nominees; winners subtracted from this gives non-winning finalists.
  • Step 4 — Revenue: each revenue stream is applied to whichever population actually buys it (winners for logo licensing/trophies/photography/PR, the larger finalist pool for finalist packages, winners plus guests for gala tickets), each at its own attach rate and price.

07 What This Deliberately Excludes

  • Entry fees — £0 throughout, per the confirmed free-to-enter model.
  • Directory listings, membership club, pay-per-lead marketplace — excluded entirely. These depend on the programme already having a credible pool of past winners and real public search traffic — neither exists in Year 1 by definition.
  • Costs of any kind — this is a revenue model only. Running 11 regional ceremonies, judging 6,820 potential slots, and building national sponsor relationships all carry substantial real costs not reflected here.

08 Sensitivity — What Moves This Most

Category fill rate is the single largest lever in the entire model: moving it from 45% to 80% roughly trebles total revenue, because it directly sets the winner count, and the winner count feeds almost every other revenue line downstream. A useful next step is a category-by-category realism check — which of the 62 categories genuinely have 5+ credible local businesses in a mid-sized county — since that answer will do more to sharpen this estimate than debating any of the pricing assumptions.

Note: this is a planning estimate built from stated assumptions, not a forecast or guarantee. It should be revisited once real data exists — actual category fill rates from the first judging round, actual attach rates from the first cohort of winners — since those figures will always be more reliable than any assumption in this report.
