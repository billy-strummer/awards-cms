1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device (for example, your computer, tablet or smartphone) when you visit the site. They can be used to recognise your device, remember your preferences and help the site function or measure how it is used.

Under UK law, including the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR) and UK GDPR, we must tell you when we use cookies, explain what they do and why, and, for most non-essential cookies, obtain your consent before they are set.

Similar technologies such as tracking pixels, tags, local storage and scripts are treated in the same way as cookies in this Policy.

2. How We Use Cookies on the British Trade Awards Website

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • make the website and British Trade Awards directory work properly (for example, to enable core navigation and security);
  • remember certain choices you make (such as cookie consent preferences);
  • understand how the site and directory are used, so we can improve them.

We keep our use of cookies to a minimum and review them periodically to ensure they remain necessary and proportionate.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function and to provide services you request. They do not require your consent but we still explain their use. Examples may include cookies that:

  • enable core page navigation and load content;
  • remember information you enter on forms during a session (for example, entry or nomination forms);
  • maintain the security of the site and help protect against malicious activity;
  • store your cookie consent choices so we do not need to ask you on every visit.

Without these cookies, parts of the website may not work properly.

3.2 Analytics and performance cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use the website, which pages are popular and how users move around the site. We use this information to improve the user experience and the way we present Awards information.

Where analytics involve storing or accessing information on your device, or collecting information that can be associated with an identifier (such as an IP address), we will only use these cookies with your consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies, which will prompt you to choose again. Depending on implementation, these may be provided by third-party services (such as analytics platforms). Where we use third-party analytics cookies, we will name the providers and explain how they use the information.

4. Cookie Consent and Control

When you first visit the British Trade Awards website, we will present a simple cookie banner that:

  • clearly explains that we use cookies and similar technologies;
  • allows you to easily “Accept All” (enabling Analytics) or “Reject Optional” (leaving Analytics disabled);
  • does not set non-essential cookies until you have made a choice.

Key points:

  • Consent must be a clear positive action – continuing to browse the site without interacting with the banner does not count as consent.
  • Rejecting non-essential cookies is as easy as accepting them.
  • You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser cookies, which will prompt the banner to appear again on your next visit.
  • Essential (strictly necessary) cookies will continue to operate even if you reject other categories, as they are required to deliver the service you request, such as loading pages or submitting forms.

5. Cookies in the British Trade Awards Forms

The British Trade Awards online forms (for entries, nominations and voting) rely on certain cookies to function securely and reliably. Examples of essential use may include:

  • maintaining a secure connection while you submit an entry or nomination;
  • remembering information you have entered within a single session so forms work as expected;
  • controlling rate-limiting and fraud-prevention mechanisms linked to voting and form use.

Any additional analytics cookies associated with the website (for example, to analyse which regions or categories are most viewed) will be treated as non-essential and only set with your consent.

6. Third-Party Cookies

Our website may include content or links from third-party services (for example, embedded maps, videos or social media plugins). These third parties may set their own cookies on your device.

We do not control these third-party cookies. We encourage you to review the cookie and privacy policies of any third-party services you interact with via our site. Where practical, we will identify the main third-party services used and link to their policies.

7. How This Cookie Policy Relates to Our Other Policies

Our Terms & Conditions explain how the British Trade Awards competition and website operate, including the website listings and liability limitations. Our Privacy Policy explains in more detail how we process personal data, including data collected via cookies and similar technologies.

This Cookie Policy focuses specifically on storage and access technologies used on your device. For more information about how we use personal data, including analytics that may be based on cookie data, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

8. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes in our use of cookies, consent mechanisms, legal requirements or guidance from the ICO.

We will publish the updated Policy on our website with a revised “last updated” date. If changes are material, we may also draw your attention to them via the cookie banner or site notices.
