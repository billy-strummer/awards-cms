2.2. To be eligible, the Entrant must have a primary place of business registered in, or substantially operate within, a county or city in England, Scotland, or Wales.

2.3. The individual submitting an entry must be aged 18 or over at the time of entry. Where an entry is submitted on behalf of a business or organisation, the authorised representative completing the submission must be aged 18 or over.
