About British Trade Awards

British Trade Awards is a competition to find the champions of construction and building services across England, Scotland and Wales – voted by the public.

From groundworks to finishing, and carpentry to plumbing, our mission is to showcase the trade professionals delivering exceptional expertise and service in their field – and in doing so, setting a new benchmark for the industry.

Launching in 2026 and spanning every county and major city across Britain, we give residential and commercial clients a trusted way to find outstanding local experts, from John o’Groats to Land’s End. Every trade. Every corner of Britain.
