About the Awards

The British Trade Awards exist to celebrate the skilled men and women who keep the nation running. From the plumber who responded at midnight to the landscaper who transformed a forgotten garden — this is the awards programme that finally gives the trades the national recognition they deserve.

Independent judging. Regional heats. A national gala ceremony. Real recognition for real craft.
