2. Eligibility

2.1 The Awards are open to businesses, sole traders and organisations operating within the construction, building and trade services sectors.

2.2 To be eligible, the Entrant must have a primary place of business registered in, or substantially operate within, a county or city in England, Scotland or Wales.

2.3 The individual submitting an entry must be aged 18 or over at the time of entry. Where an entry is submitted on behalf of a business or organisation, the authorised representative must be aged 18 or over.

2.4 Employees of the Organiser, their immediate families, sponsors, or anyone professionally connected with the Awards are not eligible to enter.

2.5 Professional Conduct: By entering, the Entrant agrees to uphold high standards of workmanship, safety and professional conduct, and to communicate clearly, honestly and lawfully with customers. The Organiser may disqualify any Entrant or revoke a Title from any Winner who brings the industry or the Awards into disrepute.

4.3 One Vote Per Person: Only one vote per individual per category is permitted. Multiple votes from the same IP address, device, email address or other identifier may be investigated and may be disqualified.

4.4 Voters may be required to provide a valid email address and any other verification information reasonably requested by the Organiser in order to verify their vote.

4.5 The Organiser may use verification tools and anti-fraud measures to assess the legitimacy of votes, including but not limited to email confirmation, CAPTCHA, rate-limiting, IP analysis, device checks, geolocation checks and duplicate detection.

4.6 Any attempt to manipulate the voting process (for example using bots, scripts, false identities, inducements or coercion) is strictly prohibited and may result in vote removal, disqualification, revocation of Titles and/or a permanent ban from future participation.
