Events Management

Plan, manage, and track award ceremonies and events

Getting Started — Events

Plan and manage your awards ceremonies and events.

1
Create an Event
Click "Create Event" below. Enter the name, date, venue, and expected capacity.
2
Set Ticket Price & Stripe (optional)
Open the event → Tickets tab. Set a price and add your Stripe key to accept online payments.
3
Share the Registration Link
Copy the Public Registration URL from the Tickets tab. Share on your website and invitations.
4
Add & Manage Attendees
Open the event → Attendees tab. Track RSVPs, assign seating, manage dietary and special requirements.
5
Issue QR Code Tickets
In the Tickets tab, click "Issue Tickets" to email QR codes to each confirmed attendee.
6
Check In Guests on the Night
Open the Check-In tab and use the QR scanner on any smartphone to mark guests as arrived.
