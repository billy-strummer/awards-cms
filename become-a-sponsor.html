Our Audience

The people behind Britain's best trade businesses

British Trade Awards attracts owners, directors, and managers of SME trade businesses — people making purchasing decisions every day. These are not passive consumers; they are active buyers of tools, materials, vehicles, insurance, finance, and software.

From sole traders entering their first awards to multi-crew contractors with national operations, our audience spans the full spectrum of Britain's £360bn construction and trade sector.
