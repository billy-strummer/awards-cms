About British Trade Awards

British Trade Awards celebrates exceptional trade and building services professionals across England, Scotland and Wales. From groundworks and roofing to fit-out and green energy, we recognise professionals delivering outstanding expertise and service in their local area.

British Trade Awards is open to businesses, sole traders and organisations operating in construction and trade services. Every size of business is welcome, from independent specialists to national contractors, provided they meet the relevant eligibility and category requirements. All entrants must be aged 18 or over. Employees of the Organiser, sponsors, and their immediate families are not eligible to enter.

British Trade Awards operates across England, Scotland and Wales, covering every county and major city. To enter, a business must be located in, or substantially operate within, the county or city for which it is applying.

Voting

The current public voting period is shown on our Key Dates and Vote sections of the website. The Organiser may extend, shorten, suspend, postpone, or cancel the voting period where reasonably necessary.

Yes. Casting a vote for your chosen trade professional or business is completely free and conducted online directly through the official British Trade Awards website.

Once nominees are announced, the public and industry can vote for their preferred trade professional or business in each category and region during the voting period. In publicly voted categories, winners are determined by a public vote using a first-past-the-post system.

You may cast one vote per person, per category. Multiple votes from the same email address, IP address, device or other identifier may be investigated and may be disqualified.

You may be asked to provide a valid email address or other information to verify your vote. This helps us maintain a fair and credible British Trade Awards process.

We may use verification and anti-fraud measures such as email confirmation, rate limiting, IP and device checks, geolocation checks, duplicate detection and audits. Votes that are incomplete, duplicated, artificially generated, unverifiable or otherwise suspicious may be removed.

Yes. Nominees are welcome to encourage genuine customers, colleagues and supporters to vote. Campaigning must be lawful, truthful and respectful. It must not suggest that voting is mandatory, guaranteed, purchased, or exchangeable for reward.

Winners & Recognition

Categories are publicly voted, and the nominee with the highest number of votes is declared the category winner, subject to verification, audit, and integrity checks. However, winners in the Industry Leadership categories may be selected by an independent panel. All decisions made by the Organiser regarding shortlisting and winners are final and binding.

Winner announcement dates are published on our Key Dates page.

Yes. Winners are invited to our champions receptions, taking place across England, Scotland and Wales — well-deserved celebrations to honour outstanding achievement.

Consumers

Consumers can use the ‘Find a Trade’ search tool on our website to source tradespeople in their area. Simply enter your County or City into the autocomplete text field and select a specific trade from the category dropdown. Clicking search will provide you with a dedicated list of vetted nominees and winners matching your location. You can also use the ‘Use my location’ button on your mobile device to automatically find professionals near you.
