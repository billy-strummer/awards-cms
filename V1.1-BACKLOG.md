## How to use this document

Nothing here is scheduled or committed — it's a prioritised list for after Version 1.0 has been live for a real stretch of time. Re-prioritise against actual usage patterns once you have them; several "Medium" items above may turn out to matter more or less than estimated once real administrators, judges, and public visitors are using the live system. The Operational Improvements category is the only one worth treating as a near-term default — everything else should wait for evidence from real usage.
