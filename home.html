Road to Recognition

How it Works

1

Entry

Trade professionals can enter their business, or clients can nominate a local expert. Entry is free and takes 3 mins.

2

Vetting

We vet every entry against strict category requirements, shortlisting only the strongest candidates for the public vote.

3

Public Voting

The public votes for their local favourites. The nominee with the most votes wins their category via a first-past-the-post system.

4

Champions Crowned

Winners are invited to one of our champions receptions to celebrate their outstanding achievement.
