System-wide Permissions: Configure role-based access control for your awards management system.
Administrator

Full system access

  • Manage all awards & winners
  • Manage organisations
  • Manage events & media
  • System settings & backups
  • User management
Editor

Content management access

  • Create & edit awards
  • Manage winners
  • Upload media
  • View reports
  • No system settings access

Read-only access

  • View awards & winners
  • View organisations
  • View events & media
  • Export reports
  • No edit permissions
